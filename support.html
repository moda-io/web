Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Email us at support@mymoda.style from the address associated with your account and we'll process the deletion.

Why am I seeing a product that's out of stock or the wrong price?
Product details come directly from our retail partners and can change without notice. The retailer's own site always has the most current price and availability.

How do purchases work?
Moda is a discovery app — when you tap through to buy something, you complete the purchase directly on the retailer's own site or app. Moda doesn't process payments or handle shipping.

I found a bug or the app crashed.
Let us know what happened and, if you can, what you were doing right before — screenshots help a lot. Email support@mymoda.style.
